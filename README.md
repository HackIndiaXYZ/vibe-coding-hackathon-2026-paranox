# vibe-coding-hackathon-2026-paranox
We are presenting BizForge -  Forge your Business
**
"BizForge- Forge your Business"** is an AI-powered business simulation platform that transforms any business idea into a living, breathing virtual world. Users define their budget, location, and vision — and the AI instantly builds a fully operational business environment complete with infrastructure, workforce, finances, and market dynamics. Make real decisions, face unpredictable events, manage resources, and watch your empire grow or collapse. Powered by intelligent AI agents acting as your Finance, HR, Marketing, and Risk advisors, the platform doesn't just simulate business — it teaches you to think like a billionaire, one decision at a time.

Here we can create, simulate and the AI will tell us that your business will is best or not, how to extend your business, what are our drawbacks, etc,..
